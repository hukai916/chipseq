## The Allo pipeline:
1. Map with Bowtie2 while allowing for multimapping
  Do not filter by MAPQ or deduplicate, let Allo adjust the BAM and MAPQ scores first.
  (Allo: https://github.com/seqcode/allo)
  Choose Bowtie2 over BWA because Bowtie2 easier to keep multi-mapped reads.
2. Output Bowtie2 results to Allo for multimapping correction
3. Call peaks with MACS3
4. Annotate peaks using TE-specific files from Telescope or SQUIRE
5. Compare with RNA-seq derived HERV expression profile
